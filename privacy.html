Privacy policy

Effective 2 November 2026. This policy covers myagentsy feeds, a private tool run by myagentsy (home).

What it accesses

Read-only access to one dedicated Gmail mailbox that the tool's owner controls (Google's gmail.readonly permission). It cannot send, change or delete email.

What it reads and keeps

It searches that mailbox for one sender's report emails and reads only the messages that search returns. From each report it keeps the figures and the identifiers needed to check them (message IDs and dates) in its owner's private database. It does not keep the emails themselves.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone. The data is used only for the owner's own business records.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the mailbox's Google Account, under Security, third-party connections.
